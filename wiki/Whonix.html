Installation

For those interested in enhancing their privacy and anonymity, installing Whonix for VirtualBox on top of Kicksecure is straightforward. This can be achieved using the pre-installed [Broken-ExtLink--no-url-was-provided], by executing the following command.

whonix-lxqt-installer-cli

For additional details and guidance, the Whonix website is a valuable resource.
