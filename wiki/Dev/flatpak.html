Bootstrapping a Trust path To Flathub Repository Signing Key

Extract Flathub Signing Key from flathub.flatpakrepo

Based on https://github.com/flatpak/flatpak/issues/5657#issuecomment-1898586690

scurl-download https://flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo

sed -ne 's/^GPGKey=//p' flathub.flatpakrepo | base64 -w0 -d | gpg --import
