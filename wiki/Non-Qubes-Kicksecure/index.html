Kicksecure means all Kicksecure platforms except Kicksecure inside Qubes. This includes Kicksecure on hardware, Kicksecure in virtual machines (VM) such as VirtualBox, and Kicksecure KVM.

  • What this page is about: Kicksecure means Kicksecure on supported platforms other than Kicksecure inside Qubes.
  • Why you might see this page: Some wiki chapters are marked "Platform specific". This means the steps can differ depending on where Kicksecure is used (for example, Kicksecure vs Kicksecure inside Qubes).
  • Which platforms are supported: See Download.
  • What is Kicksecure: See About.
  • Alternative name: Sometimes also referred to as Non-Qubes-Kicksecure.
  • What "platform" means here: "Platform" refers to where Kicksecure is used, for example directly on a computer, or inside a virtual machine (VM).
  • About Qubes OS: Kicksecure inside Qubes applies only to users of Qubes OS.
  • Easy way to tell: If you are using Qubes OS, you will know it. If you have not heard of Qubes OS before, you are almost certainly not a Qubes OS user.
