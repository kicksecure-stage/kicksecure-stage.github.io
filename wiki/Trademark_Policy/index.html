Trademark Policy

Copy or share this direct link!https://www.kicksecure.com/wiki/Trademark_Policy/#Trademark_Policy

Version: 1.0
Published: 22 February 2019

ENCRYPTED SUPPORT LLC owns a number of trademarks in both word and logo form, including brands, slogans, and styles. This policy encompasses all marks, in word and logo form, collectively referred to as "Kicksecure trademarks". You can see a non-exhaustive list of Kicksecure trademarks, including both registered and unregistered (but otherwise legally recognized) trademarks, in chapter Trademarks.

The objective of this trademark policy is:

1 to encourage widespread use and adoption of the Kicksecure trademarks,

2 to clarify proper usage of Kicksecure trademarks by third parties,

3 to prevent misuse of Kicksecure trademarks that can confuse or mislead users with respect to Kicksecure or its affiliates.

Please note that it is not the goal of this policy to limit commercial activity around Kicksecure. We encourage businesses to work on Kicksecure while being compliant with this policy.

The following are the guidelines for the proper use of Kicksecure trademarks by publishers and other third parties. Any use of or reference to Kicksecure trademarks that is inconsistent with these guidelines, or other unauthorized use of or reference to Kicksecure trademarks, or use of marks that are confusingly similar to Kicksecure trademarks, is prohibited and may violate Kicksecure trademark rights.

Any use of Kicksecure trademarks in a misleading or false manner, or in a manner that disparages Kicksecure, such as untruthful advertising, is always prohibited.

When You Can Use the Kicksecure Trademarks Without Asking Permission

Copy or share this direct link!https://www.kicksecure.com/wiki/Trademark_Policy/#When_You_Can_Use_the_Kicksecure_Trademarks_Without_Asking_Permission

1 You can use Kicksecure trademarks to make true factual statements about Kicksecure or communicate compatibility with your product truthfully.

2 Your intended use qualifies as "nominative fair use" of the Kicksecure trademarks, i.e., merely identifying that you are talking about Kicksecure in a text, without suggesting sponsorship or endorsement.

3 You can use Kicksecure trademarks to describe or advertise your services or products relating to Kicksecure in a way that is not misleading.

4 You can use Kicksecure trademarks to describe Kicksecure in articles, titles, or blog posts.

5 You can make t-shirts, desktop wallpapers, caps, or other merchandise with Kicksecure trademarks for both commercial and non-commercial usage.

6 You can also make merchandise with Kicksecure trademarks for both commercial and non-commercial usage. In case of commercial usage, we recommend that you truthfully advertise to customers which part of the selling price, if any, will be paid to the Kicksecure project. See our Donate page for more information on how to donate to the Kicksecure project.

When You Can NEVER Use the Kicksecure Trademarks Without Asking Permission

Copy or share this direct link!https://www.kicksecure.com/wiki/Trademark_Policy/#When_You_Can_NEVER_Use_the_Kicksecure_Trademarks_Without_Asking_Permission

1 You cannot use Kicksecure trademarks in any way that suggests an affiliation with or endorsement by the Kicksecure project or community, if the same is not true.

2 You cannot use Kicksecure trademarks in a company or organization name or as the name of a product or service.

3 You cannot use a name that is confusingly similar to Kicksecure trademarks.

4 You cannot use Kicksecure trademarks in a domain name, with or without commercial intent.

How to Use the Kicksecure Trademarks

Copy or share this direct link!https://www.kicksecure.com/wiki/Trademark_Policy/#How_to_Use_the_Kicksecure_Trademarks

1 Use the Kicksecure trademarks in a manner that makes it clear that your project is related to the Kicksecure project, but that it is not part of Kicksecure, nor produced by the Kicksecure project, or endorsed by the Kicksecure project.

2 Acknowledge ENCRYPTED SUPPORT LLC's ownership of the Kicksecure trademark prominently.

Example:

[TRADEMARK] is a trademark owned by ENCRYPTED SUPPORT LLC.

3 Include a disclaimer of sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement by Kicksecure on your website, as well as on all related printed materials.

Example:

X PROJECT is not affiliated with Kicksecure. Kicksecure is a trademark owned by ENCRYPTED SUPPORT LLC.

4 Distinguish the Kicksecure trademarks from the surrounding words by italicizing, bolding, or underlining them.

5 Use the Kicksecure trademarks in their exact form, neither abbreviated nor hyphenated, nor combined with any other word or words.

6 Do not create acronyms using the Kicksecure trademarks.
